GTA 6 Radio Stations

We've got some exciting news about the radio stations in GTA 6. The game is bringing back the beloved feature of in-game radio stations, and this time it's bigger and better.

One of the standout stations is Cocoteo FM, which is Leonida's home for modern Latin music. Cocoteo FM features a mix of popular genres like bachata, merengue, dembow, and reggaeton.

The station is hosted by El Martillo and La Gata, who promise to keep the party going all night long. Another station to look out for is AfroBank FM, featuring Burna and Palmsy, who curate the best in African music.

AfroBank FM covers a wide range of sounds, from classic hits to contemporary genres like amapiano and 3-step. So whether you're into Latin rhythms or African beats, there's something for everyone in the diverse lineup of radio stations in GTA 6.
