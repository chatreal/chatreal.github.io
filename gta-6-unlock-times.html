GTA 6 unlock times

Rockstar says Grand Theft Auto VI unlocks at midnight local time on 19 November 2026 on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. It is a rolling launch: New Zealand unlocks first, then each region at its own midnight.

Unlock times by region

  • New Zealand: midnight NZDT, 19 Nov (11:00 UTC on 18 Nov). First in the world.
  • Australia (Sydney, Melbourne): midnight AEDT, 19 Nov (13:00 UTC on 18 Nov).
  • Central Europe (Germany, France, Spain, Italy): midnight CET, 19 Nov (23:00 UTC on 18 Nov).
  • UK and Ireland: midnight GMT, 19 Nov (00:00 UTC).
  • US and Canada: sources disagree. Several guides say the PlayStation Store unlocks the whole US at midnight Eastern (9pm Pacific on Wednesday 18 Nov); others say midnight in each time zone. Check the game's page in your console store for the exact time.

Before it unlocks

  • Pre-load opened on 10 November. The download is up to 212 GB, so start it early and check you have the space.
  • Boxed copies still need the console store's unlock time and a large day-one download.
