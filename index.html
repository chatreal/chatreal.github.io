How it works

We choose short moments from public GTA 6 live streams, add captions, commentary and on-screen credit, and post them to our own YouTube, TikTok and Instagram accounts, alongside our own GTA 6 news and discussion videos. Clips only go to our own accounts. We do not collect, store or sell any visitor or viewer data.

Streamers: if you would like a clip removed, or you do not want your streams clipped, email chatchatrealreal@gmail.com and we will remove it and stop clipping your channel promptly.
